About EcoWell Foundation

EcoWell Foundation is a non-profit organization founded in 2025, driven by a group of enthusiastic environmentalists and health professionals who share a common vision of a sustainable and healthier future for all. Our team brings together experts from diverse backgrounds, including environmental science, public health, conservation, education, and advocacy, to tackle the multifaceted challenges facing our world today.

We Have Power Today
To Change Tomorrow!

Forests are the lungs of our planet — they clean the air we breathe, filter the water we drink, and are home to over 80% of terrestrial species. Yet, every second, a forest the size of a football field disappears. Environmental degradation and deforestation are threatening biodiversity, worsening climate change, and endangering the well-being of future generations.
